# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JS (no build step). Three.js 0.160 loaded from jsDelivr through an import map for the 3D dental arch. Preview locally with `python -m http.server 5500`.

## Users

Patients from Tatuapé and the rest of São Paulo's Zona Leste who are thinking about straightening their teeth. Confirmed mix: adults who want discreet clear aligners, parents looking for braces for their teenagers, people who want traditional fixed braces (metal or aesthetic), and patients referred for orthodontic preparation before orthognathic surgery. Most arrive from Instagram (@protteodonto) or a Google search, usually on a phone.

## Product Purpose

One-page site for Protte Odonto, the orthodontic practice of Dra. Samanta Protte. Success means the visitor books an evaluation at the practice. The SouSmile virtual pre-evaluation is a secondary path for people who are not ready to book.

## Positioning

The person who plans the case is the same specialist who sees the patient at every appointment. Dra. Samanta is a specialist in Orthodontics (not a general dentist selling aligners), examines the bite before recommending an aligner, and also handles fixed braces and orthognathic surgery preparation alongside the oral and maxillofacial surgeon.

## Operating Context

- Primary action: book an evaluation through https://linktr.ee/protteodonto.
- Secondary action: SouSmile virtual pre-evaluation (partner link with the practice's store id).
- Aligner journey: evaluation, digital scan and planning (with a simulation of the result), wearing aligners about 22 hours a day with periodic check-ups, retention.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Treatments: SouSmile clear aligners, fixed braces (metal or aesthetic), orthognathic surgery preparation, digital planning, retention and follow-up.
- Location: Tatuapé, São Paulo - SP. No street address, phone, WhatsApp number or opening hours have been provided. Do not invent them.
- Copy is written in the first person, in Dra. Samanta's voice, in Brazilian Portuguese.
- Canonical domain is still the placeholder `SEU-DOMINIO.com.br` (index.html, robots.txt, sitemap.xml).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Protte Odonto. Wordmark "PROTTE" with a yellow smile curve under it and "odontologia" below.
- Uniform colors: navy with the yellow smile.
- Professional registration shown on the site: RT CROSP 114427, CRO CL 02289.
- "Referência em alinhadores SouSmile" is the client's own claim.

## Product Principles

1. Booking an evaluation is the one primary action; everything else supports it.
2. Show the specialist, not a generic clinic: real photos, first-person voice, real registration numbers.
3. Explain the treatment honestly, including that aligners are not right for every case.
4. Stay readable and fast on a phone over mobile data.
