# Surface brief: index.html (home)

Scope: the whole one-page site. Visitor mode: Persuade. Primary action: book an evaluation (linktr.ee/protteodonto). Secondary: SouSmile virtual pre-evaluation.

Audience: Zona Leste patients on a phone, mostly from Instagram or Google: adults wanting aligners, parents of teens, fixed-braces seekers, orthognathic referrals.

Proof on hand: three real photos, CRO registrations, first-person voice. No testimonials, prices or numbers.

Constraints: keep anchor ids (topo, tratamentos, alinhadores, doutora, duvidas, contato), the 3D arch and its scroll-driven stages, JSON-LD, nothing invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is Dra. Samanta's clinical record filled in for the visitor. It proves the specialist examines every tooth and the bite before prescribing anything. It refuses both category ruts: stock smiles with tooth-icon cards, and the cream-and-italic-serif spa page this site used to be.

OWN-WORLD: Cool clinical paper (#f4f6f9) with rounded white sheets, Protte navy ink and the uniform's smile yellow used only as highlighter, primary button and live states. No sharp corners: arch hero photo, 48px sheets, pills, anatomic teeth, a round carimbo. One dark navy room for the 3D scan that opens to full width on scroll.
